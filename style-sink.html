Style sink

Every token and component from src/style.css, rendered with real content through the same renderers as the site (src/render.ts). Change the stylesheet here first, then check the full page.

Principles

  1. A gallery wall. Ink (--ink) on off-white (--wall), high contrast. Hairlines are --line, secondary text is --grey.
  2. Vermilion (--accent) marks only the most important thing: the Join call to action, the brand dot, and keyboard focus. Never decoration.
  3. Emphasis without color: invert (ink background, wall text), or go big and condensed.
  4. Two typefaces. Archivo for titles and labels (condensed, uppercase for display), Libre Caslon for reading.
  5. A 12-column grid: section labels on the left, content on the right. Generous space, asymmetric placement, numbered like a catalogue.
  6. Square corners, 1px rules, no shadows, no gradients.
  7. Images are monochrome at rest; color on hover, on focus, or while centered on screen. Portraits are the exception: colorful pop-art comics at rest, the real photo on hover.
  8. Motion is a handle, not decoration: the title steers under the pointer, content reveals once on scroll, the ticker follows your scroll. All of it switches off with reduced motion.
