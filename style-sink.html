Style sink

Every token and component from src/style.css, rendered with real content through the same renderers as the site (src/render.ts). Change the stylesheet here first, then check the full page.

Principles

  1. White on black. Lines are --dim, secondary text is --mute, everything else is --fg.
  2. Orange (--accent) marks only what we most want you to do or notice: the Join call to action, the cursor, and keyboard focus. Never decoration.
  3. Emphasis without color: invert to white background with black text.
  4. One typeface, monospace. Hierarchy comes from size, weight, and uppercase labels.
  5. Square corners, 1px rules, no shadows, no gradients.
  6. Images are monochrome at rest. Portraits are 1-bit dithered. Color appears on hover or focus.

Color

  • --bg #000
  • --fg #f4f4f4
  • --mute #8c8c8c · secondary text
  • --dim #555 · rules only, never text
  • --accent #ff5a1f · primary action, cursor, focus
