Design

- Exhibition theme: ink on an off-white gallery wall. High contrast.
- Artistic and minimal, like a gallery catalogue. Not industrial.
- Judicious use of color (vermilion), only for the most important elements to guide user's attention.
- Keep a live demo window of our latest work on the front page.
- Member portraits: colorful comic drawing by default, real photo on hover.
- Scroll effects and animation that make the site feel alive (respecting reduced motion).
- The hero title is drag and drop: letters can be pulled anywhere, tethered home by dot chains in the style of our context puck space.
- The manifesto ends with a scream: "We / are / interaction / maximalists." revealed in four chunks of increasing size, ending full screen.
- Don't duplicate information on the same screen.
- People: never leave a single person alone in a row, at any screen size.

Process

- Make sure you research the web to fetch related content and assets.
- 3 rounds:
- Round 1: Research and gather content. Output is a text only (image with placeholder) content outline draft. Focusing on information architecture and content hierarchy.
- Round 2: Design and implement the content outline draft into a working prototype. Focus on full functionality. Everything needs to work, doesn't have to look pretty.
- Rond 3: Polish the prototype to make it visually appealing. Focus on aesthetics and user experience. Make sure the design is consistent and follows the design principles mentioned above. Use a incrementally evloving style sink to steer the stylesheet authoring
